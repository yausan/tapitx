The Vision of Karma

Founded by Yausan Adhikari (Karma) • Kathmandu, Nepal 🇳🇵

Karma noticed how millions of students and young gamers in Nepal spend hours playing mobile games daily without anything to show for it. Struggling with limited pocket money, their time was wasted. That led him to ask:

“Why should gaming only waste your time when your skills and focus can help you earn real money and build financial independence?”

That question sparked the creation of TapIt—a platform designed around Play & Earn. TapIt gives students in Nepal and worldwide a legitimate, fun way to earn pocket money while sharpening their mental reflexes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TapIt completely free to earn? +
Yes, TapIt is 100% free. There are no registration fees, deposit requirements, or hidden costs. Download from Google Play Store, play games, and cash out your rewards.
How do redemptions work in Nepal? +
Your earned rewards are credited instantly to your cloud wallet. You can redeem directly to eSewa, Khalti, Mobile Recharge, or global options like PayPal.
Which app stores are supported? +
TapIt is available right now on the Google Play Store for Android devices (v6.0+). The Apple App Store version for iOS is in final review and coming soon!
How does TapIt support student growth? +
TapIt provides a productive part-time opportunity for students to earn pocket money while sharpening their mental agility, focus, and reaction reflexes.
